Privacy Policy

Starcruise Frontiers · last updated 3 November 2026

Starcruise Frontiers is an online multiplayer strategy game, currently in a closed alpha. There is no account, no login, and no email address. The game runs on a game server, so your progress is stored there rather than on your phone. This page lists everything that server keeps.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No advertising identifier, IDFA or IDFV.
  • No location, precise or approximate.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. The app never asks.
  • No payment details. The alpha has no purchases.

Your player identity

The first time you open the game, the server gives you an anonymous player identity: a long random number and a token that proves it is yours. The token is stored in your device's Keychain so you keep the same base when you reopen the app. It is not derived from your hardware and is not linked to any account. iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is deleted, so reinstalling on the same device usually brings back the same player. This is what Apple calls Identifiers → User ID.

What the game server stores

  • Your game. Your base and its position on the map, buildings, resources, squads and their marches, outposts you hold, battle results, descent runs and their scores, and how far you are through the opening tutorial. (User Content → Gameplay Content.)
  • Sessions. When each session starts and ends, the app version, and the platform (for example iPhone). We use this to count how many players come back. (Usage Data → Product Interaction.)
  • Error reports. When the app hits an error, it sends the error message, where in the code it happened, and the app version: at most 20 per player per hour. These messages come from the game's own code and contain no personal content. (Diagnostics → Other Diagnostic Data.)

Each of these is stored against your anonymous player identity. None of it is used for advertising or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

What other players can see

The map is shared. Other players can see your base, your squads and their marches, and the outposts you hold, together with your anonymous player identity. Your buildings, resources, descent runs, sessions and error reports are private: only you and the operator can see them. Battle reports are visible only to the two players involved.

Where the data is held

The game server runs on SpacetimeDB Maincloud, a hosting service operated by Clockwork Labs, which stores the game database on our behalf. Our database does not store your IP address. The hosting service handles network connections under its own policy.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDK.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK from anyone else. The reports above go only to our own game server.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, which is why the app shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.

How long we keep it

For the length of the alpha. If the alpha world is reset, testers are told beforehand, and everything listed above is erased with it.

Children

The alpha is open only to invited testers and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

To have your player and everything stored against it deleted, email the address below. If you can, include the short player code shown at the top of the game screen. We can still act without it if you tell us roughly when you played.

Changes

If what the game collects changes, this page changes with it and the date above moves. The App Store privacy label is based on the same list.
