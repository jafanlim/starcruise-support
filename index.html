Starcruise — Support

A word puzzle that travels from low Earth orbit to real star systems.

Get in touch

Email akostnz@gmail.com. Please include your device, your iOS version, and the build number — Settings → About inside the app shows the version and build.

Common questions

  • My progress disappeared. Progress is stored on the device and can also sync through your own iCloud. Make sure iCloud Drive is on for Starcruise. If a level you were part-way through reset, tell us the level number.
  • I bought boost cells and they are missing. Settings → Restore purchases. Restoring returns purchases, not cells already spent.
  • Haptics stopped working. Check Settings inside the app, and that Low Power Mode is off — iOS disables haptics in Low Power Mode.
  • Do I need to be online? No. The game is fully playable offline.
  • Are the facts real? Yes — destinations, distances and the propulsion tech tree are drawn from published sources.

Privacy

The app collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.
