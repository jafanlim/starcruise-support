Privacy Policy

Starcruise · last updated 2 September 2026

Starcruise is a single-player word puzzle. There is no account, no login, and no server of ours that stores anything about you. Most of what the app knows stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No device identifier, advertising identifier, IDFA or IDFV.
  • No location, precise or approximate.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access — the app never asks.
  • No words you find, and no puzzle content you produce.

What we do collect

Anonymous usage events, so we can see which parts of the game work and which do not. Each event is a name, a timestamp and a few non-identifying numbers drawn from the game itself — for example that a level started, that a hint was used and which kind, how long a level took, or that a purchase screen was opened.

Events are grouped by a random identifier generated on your device the first time you play. It is not derived from your hardware, it is not linked to any account, it is stored with the app's own data, and deleting the app destroys it. Reinstalling produces a new one. This is what Apple calls Usage Data → Product Interaction, not linked to you.

Events are processed by PostHog on our behalf. The project is configured to discard client IP addresses, so no network address is retained. No analytics SDK is embedded in the app; it posts a small JSON object to PostHog's documented endpoint and nothing else.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no advertising SDK.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, which is why the app shows no App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No profiling, and no automated decisions about you.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app stores only Apple's transaction identifiers, on your device, so a purchase can be restored.

Game Center and iCloud

Achievements use Apple's Game Center, and only if you are signed in to it — that relationship is between you and Apple. Your progress can sync through your own iCloud account so it survives changing devices. That data is yours and sits in your iCloud, not on any server of ours.

Children

Starcruise is rated 4+. It has no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content and no web browsing, and it collects nothing that identifies anyone of any age.

Your choices

Deleting the app removes the random identifier and everything stored on the device. To have events already received deleted, email the address below and say so — including the identifier if you can still see it, though we can act without it.

Changes

If what the app collects changes, this page changes with it and the date above moves. The App Store privacy label is derived from the same list.
